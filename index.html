How to play

  • Steer with your mouse. Point near your bean to creep, far away to run flat out.
  • Eat to grow. Jellybeans on the floor make you bigger. Any bean smaller than you is food — anything bigger, run.
  • Dash and grab power-ups. Space lunges forward. Power-ups give you speed, shields, magnets and bombs; F throws what you're holding.
  • Be the last bean standing. Wins pay coins and gems for chests, skins, trails and victory poses.

Four ways to play

Pick a mode with the arrows under your bean on the home screen.

Classic

Eat beans, eat rivals, be the last bean standing — with random arena events like jellybean rain and giant gumballs.

Survivor

Survive 20 waves of angry gummies. Your weapons fire by themselves — you pick the upgrades.

Infection

One bean is a zombie — maybe you. Tag everyone, or outrun them until the clock runs out.

Hide & Seek

Lights out. Hide in bushes and vents, or be the seeker with a flashlight and find them all.

Collect it all

  • Skins, hats, patterns and colours from treasure chests.
  • Trails that follow you round the arena, and victory poses for when you win.
  • Challenges unlock the rarest items by skill, never by luck — a win streak, a win without dashing, ten wins.
  • Sets of four themed items pay out gems when you finish them. The collection book keeps score.

Good to know

Does my progress save?

Yes, in this browser on this computer. Make an account in the game, or play as a guest. Clearing your browser's data clears it too.

How does full screen work?

Press the full screen button under the game. Esc leaves full screen (in Chrome, hold it — a quick press pauses the game). P pauses too.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on a laptop or desktop. A mouse and keyboard work best.

Can we play together?

This page plays offline against bots. Playing online with friends needs someone running the game's server — the desktop version can host one.

It's stuck loading.

The first load downloads the whole game. On a slow network give it a minute, then reload the page. After that it loads faster.

It's running slowly.

Close other tabs, then open the game's settings (the gear on the home screen) and turn off High detail and Smooth edges.
